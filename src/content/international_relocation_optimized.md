# International Relocation Checklist: What to Do 3 Months Out to Move-In

**Quick answer:** Start visa and document work 3 months ahead — it's the step most likely to delay a move. Book an international shipper 6-8 weeks out, since ocean freight typically takes 4-8 weeks in transit. Secure housing before you land where possible, since most landlords abroad won't lease sight unseen without a local guarantor or extra deposit. Full timeline below.

## 3 months before moving abroad
- Confirm visa/work permit requirements and start applications — processing time is the single biggest driver of relocation delays
- Get certified copies of key documents: birth certificate, marriage certificate, academic transcripts, vaccination records
- Research healthcare requirements and whether your destination requires proof of insurance to enter

## 6-8 weeks before moving abroad
- Book an international shipper or freight forwarder; ocean freight generally takes 4-8 weeks in transit, air freight 1-2 weeks at higher cost
- Decide what to ship vs. sell vs. store — shipping costs are typically calculated by volume, not weight
- Start the housing search; ask specifically whether the market expects a local guarantor or larger security deposit from newcomers

## 2-4 weeks before moving abroad
- Notify your home country bank and any subscriptions of your move; check whether your accounts stay accessible from abroad
- Arrange interim health insurance covering the transition period between home-country and destination-country coverage
- Get an international driving permit if you plan to drive immediately on arrival

## What documents do I need for an international relocation?
Passport with 6+ months validity, visa/work permit, certified birth and marriage certificates, academic and vaccination records, and proof of funds where required. Bring physical certified copies — many countries won't accept scans for official registration.

## How do I ship my belongings when moving to another country?
Get quotes from at least two international freight forwarders. Ocean freight (4-8 weeks) is far cheaper per volume than air freight (1-2 weeks) — most movers ship non-essentials by sea and pack a smaller air-shipped or checked-luggage set of immediate needs.

## How far in advance should I plan an international move?
3 months minimum, driven almost entirely by visa processing time, not packing. Housing and shipping can compress into 6-8 weeks if visa work is already done.

*Timelines reflect typical ranges for international relocations as of 2026; visa processing time varies significantly by destination country and visa category.*
